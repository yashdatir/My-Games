The Ten Drops of Amrita

A drop of divine nectar still burns in ten sacred sanctums. The Eclipse-Lord Svarbhanu wants every one of them. Take up your bow, awaken the sky-chariot, and fly to the stars to stop him.

  1. The Churning. When the gods and asuras churned the Ocean of Milk, ten drops of Amrita fell across Jambudweep. The Rishis raised a sanctum over each and sealed it with a golden Dhwaja.
  2. The Eclipse. Svarbhanu, the demon who once stole a sip of nectar, has returned. His Chhaya-Sena, an army of living shadow, now holds the sanctums, and his Grahan-Durg (eclipse fortress) darkens the sky.
  3. Your Dharma. As the last Amrit-Rakshak, begin in Suryapuri. Free the sanctums, wake the Garuda Vimana, rise beyond the clouds and break the Grahan-Durg before the sun goes dark.
